## Built around healthcare

Medtrica manufactures durable and disposable medical products for hospitals, clinics, surgery centers and long-term care facilities. Our focus is patient comfort, practical design and the purchasing needs of healthcare teams.

## From Canada, serving North America

Our manufacturing operations are based in Canada, with sales offices in Surrey, British Columbia, and the Los Angeles area. Our range spans mattresses, surgical and imaging pads, positioning products, and endoscopy supplies.

## A fit for your facility

Standard products are only part of the picture. We also discuss custom dimensions and component requirements with facilities and manufacturing partners.
