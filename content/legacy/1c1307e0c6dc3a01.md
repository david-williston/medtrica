Let us have a look at some ways in which these pillows help patients as well as the doctors and caretakers.</p><p><strong>Utmost comfort during the examination</strong></p><p>It is crucial for the patient to be in a comfortable position throughout the investigation or procedures like x-rays, surgeries, CT scans, and MRIs. As compared to standard cushions and pillows, these medical-grade pillows are more stable and offer improved comfort to the patients.</p><p><strong>Ideal for post-treatment care</strong></p><p>There are many different types of medical procedures, surgeries, and treatments after which the patients are required to lie in a particular position to help accelerate the healing process. Available in many different shapes and sizes, the medical positioning pillows/pads can be very useful in such cases.</p><p><strong>Help prevent decubiti and sacral pressure ulcers</strong></p><p>Decubiti or sacral pressure ulcers are formed over the skin of the patients due to prolonged pressure and immobility, especially in bedridden patients.
