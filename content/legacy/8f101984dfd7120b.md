For people who are confined to bed for a longer period, the medical mattresses are even more important. They help relieve pressure points by cushioning areas prone to developing bedsores. However, people who are overweight or are medically obese </span><span>require special <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bariatrics">bariatric</a> bed mattresses</span><span>. <li><ul>
<li><span>Bariatric bed mattresses</span><span><span><span> can support heavy weights of more than 300 kilos. They feature a deep base and foam that is firm enough to prevent the body from sinking in too deep. They may have additional layers for comfort and extra support.</span></span></span></li></ul>
</li></ul><ul>
<li><ul>
<li><span><span><span>Pressure relief is important when lying in bed especially for overweight individuals. Painful bedsores can develop on bony protrusions of the body. A bariatric mattress such as that available at Medtrica is built to prevent or at least alleviate the condition. It distributes weight more evenly so that pressure is taken off any one part of the body.</span></span></span></li></ul>
</li></ul><ul>
<li><ul>
<li><span><span><span><span>Proper air circulation is important for the prevention of bedsores. A bariatric mattress helps manage moisture as it uses fabrics that wick sweat away and keep skin dry. People can sleep more soundly and suffer fewer discomforts.</span></span></span></span></li>
<li><span><span><span>These bariatric products come in different sizes and weight capacities to accommodate most standard bed frames. However, they can also be custom ordered to fit special bed frames.</span></span></span></li></ul>
