<li><h3><strong><span>Benefits In Pain Relief For Joints And Spine</span></strong></h3>
</li></ul><p>NASA developed Tempur-Pedic mattress technology with the use of viscoelastic or polyurethane material to keep the temperature of the mattress comforting. The mattress made up of such materials are capable of supporting your body better than anything else. The joints which experience the pain more often are well-taken care off so that you can sleep pain-free. Plus, unlike the standard <a href='{{< sitepath "product-category/hospital-bed-mattress/overlay-mattress-pads/" >}}'>memory foam mattresses</a>, they are capable of regulating the temperature according to the external environment so that you wake up sweating in the middle of the night.</p><ul>
<li><h3><strong><span>Benefits In Spine Alignment And Protection</span></strong></h3>
</li></ul><p>The Tempur-Pedic mattress toppers and mattresses act as a cradle for your back which hugs your spine rather than just providing it a surface to rest on.</p><p>If you have been through the madness of the day and are looking forward to a good night’s sleep, the Tempur-Pedic mattress is great to keep your neck and spine aligned. This will help you wake up in the morning with a fresh mind.</p><p>These are even mattresses, and therefore, they are less likely to develop depressions with your weight, developing dents or holes in the middle of the bed. This makes lying on the bed a horrible experience for the spinal cord.</p><p>Apart from the mattresses, you can also opt for Tempur-Pedic pillows to support your neck well. They are equally resilient and keep your neck in a comfortable position. Additionally, these pillows are also available in all the standard sizes so that you can select the size you habitually use.</p><ul>
<li><h3><strong><span>No More Motion Transfer</span></strong></h3>
</li></ul><p>When you are sharing your bed with your partner, it becomes challenging to sleep in peace especially if your partner’s turns and tosses in sleep a lot. Motion transfer is the villain here. Then again, you can opt for a good quality Tempur-Pedic mattress which creates a separate cradle for everyone lying on the bed so that your partner’s movement is not transferred to you. Plus, this will work wonders for your partner’s restlessness as well.</p><ul>
<li><h3><strong><span>Allergy Relief</span></strong></h3>
</li></ul><p>Tempur-Pedic mattress covers and mattresses, like other contemporaries like memory foam mattresses, are hypoallergenic. Therefore, they do not attract and retain dust mites and germs which can prove fatal for your family in the allergy season. And, their design and build facilitate complete comfort so that you can sleep deeply. By making you a deep sleeper, it prevents waking up with watery eyes, congestion, and migraine.</p><ul>
