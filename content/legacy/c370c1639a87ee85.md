<div><h2><strong>What Are Hospital Bed Air Mattresses?</strong></h2>
<p>A hospital bed air mattress is an alternating pressure mattress system developed for the comfort of the patients under-recovery, confined to the bed for more than 15 hours in a day. Such patients are likely to develop bedsores which later develop into pressure ulcers. These bed mattress helps to prevent pressure ulcers. To avoid skin breakdown in the patients due to low blood flow, medical air mattress uses alternating pressure and low air loss therapy to stimulate the circulation in the patient’s body.</p>
<p>The mattress<a href='{{< sitepath "product-category/hospital-bed-mattress/overlay-mattress-pads/" >}}'> </a>overlays are filled with air, and consist of several air cylinders placed parallel to each other. Together, these cylinders make for an air-filled mattress. They are typically made up of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyvinyl_chloride">Polyvinyl Chloride (PVC)</a> and, nowadays, textile-reinforced Urethane Plastic versions also exist. The <a href='{{< sitepath "media/cb0730fbb655-download" >}}'>material has the strength and sturdiness to support up to 350 pounds of weight</a>. The accompanying digital pump can be used to inflate and deflate it and control the air pressure.</p>
<p>The most preferred deflating air <a href='{{< sitepath "product-category/hospital-bed-mattress/" >}}'>mattress for hospital bed</a> is made up of water-resistant material, has a vapour-permeable surface, and comes with a soft quilt-like feel to it.</p>

<h2><strong>Why are Air Mattresses Required? </strong></h2>
<p>Restricted blood flowing in the body of patients who are bedridden or the ones who have lost mobility breaks down the skin cells. This skin cell breakdown can result in bedsores which later convert into painful ulcers. Hospital air bed mattress is, therefore, a prerequisite to help keep patients comfortable and prevent them from the various degree of risks.</p>
<h2><strong>Benefits Of Using Hospital Bed Air Mattress</strong></h2>
<p>When any of your loved ones are dealing with medical issues, your priority is to ensure their comfort. This is where the hospital bed air mattress comes into role. Experts suggest these uniquely designed mattresses to patients for the utmost comfort and quick recovery. A few benefits of these products are:</p>
<ul>
<li>Improved Blood Circulation: As the patient moves on the mattress, the pressure points keep on alternating. This further assists in enhancing the blood circulation of the patient’s body. They also feel fresh while sleeping on the mattress due to the changing pressure points. Therefore, they obtain more sound sleep on the bed.</li>
<li>Alternate Pressure Points: Patients who spend extended hours on their beds are prone to develop problems like bed sores and pressure ulcers. This happens due to continuous pressure on specific points of their body. However, the problem can be avoided with air mattress for bed sores because of their alternating pressure point feature.</li>
<li>Low-Air Loss Technology: Hot surfaces can be challenging to sleep on. That is why an air mattress for a hospital bed is incorporated with a low-air loss technology. Under this, numerous small holes are provided on the mattress. These holes let a small amount of air leak and keep the surface cool for sleeping.</li>
<li>Convenience: Mattresses used for patients require frequent cleaning and maintenance. If you opt for a regular heavy mattress for this purpose, this task will become hectic. But there is no such problem with medical air mattress as they are lightweight and easy to maintain. So neither the patient nor the caregiver will have any issue.</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>How Do Air Mattresses For Hospital Bed Work?</strong></h2>
<p><img alt="Hospital Air Mattress" decoding="async" loading="lazy" src='{{< sitepath "media/358536d890ef-Hospital-Air-Mattress-120x300.png" >}}'/></p>
<p>As mentioned above, the air cylinders in the alternating pressure mattresses are placed parallel to each other. The air moves in a sequence from one cylinder to another to perfuse the blood in areas which are at higher risk of developing pressure ulcers. When one cell gets inflated, the other one gets deflated. This motion of air within the pneumatic mattress relieves the pressure on problematic areas of the body regularly, stimulating blood flow.</p>
