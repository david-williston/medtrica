<li>High repair costs</li>
<li>Potential patient risks</li></ul><p><strong> Preventive measures </strong></p><p>To prevent damages, the endoscope should be stored as per the manufacturer’s recommendations. Endoscopes suffer various damages when cleaning and transporting them.</p><p><strong>Long Term Protective Solution </strong></p><p>Protecting the tip and the tube using protective solutions is the best way to keep the instrument safe. Endoscope tip protectors reduce the chances for damage due to impact. The protectors are used at all times before and after a procedure including the manual wash. Medtrica’s tip protectors do not allow for moisture build-up in the instrument or around the lens, (If moisture does build up it can result in bacterial growth).</p><p>An endoscope tip protector provides protection to the tip during transport, storage and cleaning. The protector is made up of material that is not abrasive so it does not scratch the lens of the instrument. Its characteristics include:</p><ul>
<li>It fits the tips of the endoscope with ease</li>
<li>It provides a safe, simple, and effective way of protecting the tip</li>
<li>Since it is made of non-porous expandable mesh, it leaves room for the tip to breathe thus eliminating the danger of accumulation of moisture</li>
