Insomniacs generally get a restless sleep with frequent disturbances during the night.</span></p><p><span>Sleep-related issues associated with fibromyalgia don’t stop at insomnia. People with fibromyalgia have also complained about suffering from restless leg syndrome. It is the constant urge to have to move your legs around while you’re in bed. It’s aggravating and can keep you up all night long. You always feel that your legs aren’t able to be in a comfortable position.</span></p><p><span>Another problem that comes along with this condition is sleep apnea.
