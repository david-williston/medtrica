<figure></figure><p>An overlay mattress provides an added layer of comfort to the body that comes recommended for patients undergoing physiotherapy, recuperating from a back surgery or those with weak joints. At hospitals, an overlay pad plays a pivotal role in making sure that a patient receives sound sleep without worrying about bed sores.</p><p><strong>Overlay pads come in various thicknesses.</strong></p><p>Commercially, <a href='{{< sitepath "product-category/hospital-bed-mattress/overlay-mattress-pads/" >}}'>overlay mattress pads</a> come in 2”, 3” and 4” thickness, but these can also be custom-made as per the health care institution’s requirements. For most cases, 3” thickness works just fine.</p><p><strong>Responsive to body temperature.</strong></p><p>A mattress overlay for bed sores responds to body heat and only softens around the area where a person rests, while the rest of the mattress remains firm.</p><p><strong>Aids in a speedy recovery.</strong></p><p>One of the best benefits that come from having an overlay pad is that it aids in speedy recovery after a surgery or an injury, at the time when a patient’s body is the most delicate. An overlay mattress provides comfort and relaxation, which are the key to recovery</p><p><strong>Even supports heavy individuals.</strong></p><p>Not only <a href='{{< sitepath "product-category/hospital-bed-mattress/bariatric-mattress/" >}}'>bariatric mattresses</a> but the effectiveness of 4” mattress overlay pads of the correct foam construction also prevents bed sores among heavy individuals.</p>  <div><div><p>
