<div><h2><strong><span>What is a Therapeutic Mattress?</span></strong></h2>
<p>Therapeutic mattress helps troubled sleepers get a good night’s rest. Those who find it hard to get a satisfying rest at night, toss and turn quite a bit or often wake up in a sweat, will find comfort and relaxation with a therapeutic mattress. Although someone who has suffered a grave injury or is medically advised to stay in bed for an extended period must also use a therapeutic bed mattress for a speedy recovery.</p>
<p>Not all medical facilities are equipped with a therapeutic bed mattress and neither are all therapeutic mattresses designed the same way, but upgrading to this scientifically designed modern marvel has numerous health benefits.</p>
<h2><strong><span>Here are the 5 Benefits of Therapeutic Bed Mattress<br/>
</span></strong></h2>
<p>Therapeutic mattress is indeed your way forward to a mesmerizing sleep in these times when stress overrules all. They are incredibly comfortable and contribute to the healing of patients as well.</p>

<p>Depending on a distinct medical condition, a patient may be prescribed one of the many forms of a therapeutic bed mattress – memory foam, air, water, and gel. Furthermore, these mattresses are sub-categorized as foam, low air loss, alternating pressure, lateral rotation, and bariatric. Each one of them serves a distinct purpose, yet all of them offer maximum comfort and relaxation to the patient.</p>
<p>While memory foam mattresses are perfect for sleepers who are looking for a mattress to provide them regular comfort, the air loss mattresses turn your bed into not only a therapeutic bed, but also a healing bed. The circulation of air within its cells gives perfect support to the pressure points of the patients who are at risk of developing bedsores or skin ulcers.</p>

<h3><strong>Firmness of the Different Foam Layers and Zones Help to Prevent Bed Sores</strong></h3>
<p><span>Therapeutic Bed Mattress Prevent Bed Sores — image unavailable on the source website.</span></p></li>

<p>Unlike spring coil mattresses, a therapeutic bed mattress has different memory foam layers and zones of foam firmness and densities which do not sag. In retrospect, it conforms to the curves of a human body while not casting a deep impression into the foam either. Actually, these mattresses respond to a body’s heat dissipation and only stay soft within that zone.</p>
<p>Besides, therapeutic bed memory foam mattresses that run on-air pressure mechanism make sure that, at no point, the pressure falls on one part of the body for long. With the pressure being alternated through therapeutic mattresses, the blood circulation and temperature regulation of the body also improves, catering to the healing needs of patients who are at a high risk of developing or have already developed bedsores.</p>
