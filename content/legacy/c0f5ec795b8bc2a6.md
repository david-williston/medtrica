<figure></figure><p>In order to ensure the overall hygiene and longevity of the hospital mattresses, it is essential to use high-quality covers. Since it involves a lot of investment, buying a well-fitted mattress for hospital beds needs careful research.</p><p>You might not need to <strong><a href='{{< sitepath "product-category/hospital-bed-mattress/" >}}'>buy a mattress cover for your hospital beds</a> </strong>frequently, but it sure does involve careful consideration of various factors. Ultimately, your aim should be to make sure your patients are comfortable while keeping the expenses tab under control.</p><p>So here are the main features that you need to look for while buying fitted covers for the hospital beds.</p><ul>
<li><strong>Hypoallergenic</strong></li></ul><p>The best hospital mattress covers are made of hypoallergenic materials so that anyone including people with allergic tendencies can use them safely.</p><ul>
<li><strong>Ease of maintenance</strong></li></ul><p>Choose a fabric that does not stain easily and is simple to maintain. 70D Nylon, 4 way stretch and vinyl covers for hospital beds are ideal, since these require the least efforts for maintenance, and last long too.</p><ul>
<li><strong>Covers with zipper</strong></li></ul><p>Mattress covers that come with zippers are easiest to maintain. You can easily take off the cover when you need to wash it and put it back on the mattress. There will be no need for you to struggle through the process.</p><ul>
<li><strong>Stretchable fabric</strong></li></ul><p>A <a href='{{< sitepath "product-category/hospital-bed-mattress/mattress-covers/" >}}'><strong>fitted mattress cover</strong></a> should ideally be made out of fabrics that can offer a reasonable degree of “give”. Mattresses of varied sizes can be easily fit into stretchable covers.</p><ul>
<li><strong>Brand</strong></li></ul><p>Make sure you choose a reliable brand for the best quality of covers for your requirements. But at the same time strike the correct balance with your budget.</p><ul>
<li><strong>Warranty </strong></li></ul><p>Look for the manufacturer’s warranty to cover you after you have made the purchase. If the terms are unclear, make sure to ask and clarify them all.</p><p>With various brands selling their products on the market today, buying fitted or full clam shell mattress covers can seem like a confusing task. But when you keep this quick buying guide in focus, you will easily be able to find out the best product for your needs.</p>  <div><div><p>
