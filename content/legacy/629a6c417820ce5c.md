They are specially used for patients who are bed-ridden and need support to sit at an angle. You can lounge on the mattress in different positions; elevate your back or legs, for better relaxation.</p><p>People suffering from frequent heart burns, asthma, poor blood circulation, snoring, and sleep apnea can use adjustable beds to keep their body elevated while sleeping and avoid troubles. Air adjustable beds facilitate air movement within, alleviating strain from pressure points, promoting the blood flow in the body, and reducing the risk of shear and friction.</p><p><strong>Also Read:</strong> <strong><a href='{{< sitepath "explain-briefly-use-hospital-air-mattress/" >}}'>Explain Briefly About the Use of Hospital Air Mattress</a></strong></p><h2><strong>Benefits of Adjustable Beds Mattress </strong></h2><p>Mattress for adjustable bed offers a multitude of benefits not only for the patients but regular sleepers as well.</p><ul>
<li><p>A viable option for the patients suffering from sleep apnea, acid reflux, heaving snoring, sinus congestion, asthma, and other such issues. By keeping their heads and neck elevated, they can allow their windpipe to be in a relaxed position, saving them from the flattening and strain on the passage.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Air adjustable mattresses allow air to circulate within their cells so that the patients can sleep too. Apart from temperature regulation, they alleviate pressure from the pressure points of the patients’ body, reducing the risk of developing a pressure sore.</p>
</li>
<li><p>They not only provide comfort to the patients but contribute to their healing as well because the increased blood circulation allows the skin cells to renew. This, in turn, results in quicker healing of patients who were earlier suffering from uncomfortable bed sores.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Keeping your feet elevated on an adjustable bed will have a positive effect on your blood circulation immediately and helps you get rid of swelling in feet, ankles, and legs.</p>
</li>
<li><p>The risk of chronic lung diseases and cardiovascular disorders can also be reduced by keeping the upper body elevated while sleeping.</p>

Also, it is a long-lasting material.</p><p>A hybrid mattress has the bottom innerspring layer, topped with a layer of memory foam. The combination of the two makes the bed affordable, and the spring coils last longer than a memory foam base.</p><p>While memory foam mattresses retain heat, you can make them temperature regulating like adjustable bed air mattresses by adding adjustable air mattress toppers on them.</p><ol>
<li><h3><strong>Best Latex Mattress for Adjustable Beds</strong></h3>
</li></ol><p><span>Latex Mattress — image unavailable on the source website.</span></p><p>An all latex mattress is everything that you need for your adjustable bed because it is super flexible, provides the bounce you are looking for, and conforms to that shape of the body well. Apart from that, they do not heat up as much as memory foam does. However, if you feel that you can do with a layer of natural fibre mattress on top, you can add an adjustable bed mattress pad to it.</p><p>The natural bounce, adaptability, and softness of the latex mattresses have made them quite popular amongst the masses quickly. Even the adjustable bed air mattresses have latex covering to enhance durability.</p><ol>

</li></ul><h2><strong>How to Pick Adjustable Beds Mattress? </strong></h2><h3>Here are a few steps you can follow to pick the right adjustable bed mattress.</h3><h3><strong>Step 1: Check if you need a New Mattress</strong></h3><p>Since mattresses can be heavy investments, first you need to check if your mattress is older than eight years and quite worn out, then you can think about replacing it. Also, check whether your mattress is still under warranty and you can get it replaced.</p><p>However, if your health issues have made it impossible for you to sleep on a regular mattress, you can invest in another one whatsoever.</p><h3><strong>Step 2:  Decide which type of Mattress you need</strong></h3><p>According to the health condition of the patient, you can decide upon which kind of mattress you want to invest in. If the patient is at a very high risk of developing bed sores, you need an adjustable bed air mattress. Whereas for regular users who suffer from frequent refluxes, sleep apnea, and heavy snoring, even <a href='{{< sitepath "product-category/memory-foam-mattress/" >}}'>memory foam mattresses</a>, latex mattress, or hybrid mattress will do the job.</p><h3><strong>Step 3: Stick to your Budget</strong></h3><p>When you are looking for an adjustable bed mattress, we would advise you to have a budget and stick to it because they can be pretty expensive. If you have a tight budget, you can stick to hybrid mattresses that are a combination of innerspring and memory foam.</p><p>Also, you can add an adjustable air mattress topper on your regular mattress for comfort if you are on a strict budget.</p><h3><strong>Step 4: Firmness of the Mattress</strong></h3><p>Firmness is how soft you want your mattress to feel when you sleep on it. If you have frequent backache issues, you can opt for a firmer mattress. Whereas, you can opt for multiple-layered mattresses when you are looking for a softer feel from a firm base.</p><p>If you wake up sore in the morning even when you slept on the right side of the bed, something is indeed wrong with your mattress.
