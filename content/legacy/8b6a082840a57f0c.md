<li><p>Innerspring hospital mattresses are the most economical choice. They are made of evenly distributed inter-mattress coils that make it either soft or rather firm.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Foam hospital mattresses are lighter in weight as compared to innerspring but are also economical. They do absorb shock from motion.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Low air loss hospital mattresses are suitable for low mobility patients with bedsores. They reduce skin moisture by reducing pressure on the weight-bearing areas.</p>
</li>
<li><p>Alternating pressure hospital mattresses come with a benefit of the air mattress while aiding in circulation.</p>

</li>
<li><h3><strong>Patient Size:<br/>
</strong></h3><p>Hospital mattresses come in an array of lengths for accommodating patients of various lengths. Always choose the mattress that is longer than the patient is.</p>
</li>
