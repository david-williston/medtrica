They offer many benefits to both patients and medical professionals alike. Learn more about what these are.</p><p>Performing medical procedures like urology treatment requires patients to be comfortable and in position. Implements must be sterilized and the right tools must be available to doctors. One of the more commonly used items is table pad covers. They are easily missed by a layperson but healthcare providers know just how important they are. Check out why they are needed.</p><h2><strong>Enhances Comfort</strong></h2><p>Table pad covers, as the name suggests, cover examination tables. While it would seem that ordinary vinyl would suffice, medical pads and covers are designed differently. For starters, these pads have varying thicknesses that provide different levels of comfort. The covers are stretchable and made to reduce pressure points.</p><h2><strong>Ergonomically Designed</strong></h2><p>X-ray pads and other table pads, such as those available at Medtrica, are built of medical grade foam that has a high density for adequate firmness. They feature a no- or low-slip bottom to keep them in place. The pads are easy to maintain too. Since spillage could occur during use, they are stain-proof and can be cleaned with little trouble. The seams are sealed as well to prevent fluid from leaking in.</p><h2><strong>Allows for Table Extension</strong></h2><p>Along with table pads and covers, table extension pads are frequently used in imaging facilities and doctors clinics. They are attached to the headrest and footrest depending on the requirement. They are extremely useful, as they add to a patient’s comfort and negate the need for purchasing additional larger tables.</p><p><a href='{{< sitepath "product-category/x-ray-table-pads/" >}}'>X-ray pads</a> can be made of various materials. They can be foam or gel with the latter designed to be cooled and heated. The pads may feature handles to allow for easier patient transfer and reduce the strain on personnel.</p><p><a href='{{< sitepath "product/medical-imaging-x-ray-table-strap/" >}}'>Table extension pads</a> are essential for any clinic, hospital, or healthcare center. Along with pad covers, they provide patients with a comfortable surface to rest on while undergoing medical examinations. Doctors and assistants benefit too, as they are able to shift patients from bed to gurney and back safely and easily.</p>  <div><div><p>
