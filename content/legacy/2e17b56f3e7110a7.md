<figure></figure><p>Sandbags that are manufactured for medical purposes are used extensively in hospitals and nursing homes for both pre as well as the post-operative welfare of patients. Medical sandbags are important equipment which are used by both doctors and physiotherapists during the treatment of their patients.</p><p>In the healthcare industry, the medical sandbags are commonly used for a variety of purposes, starting from rehabilitation of patients to physiotherapy and even for maintaining the position of a patient during a surgery. Sandbags meant for medical use, are also used to stop bleeding and promote coagulation after a patient has undergone a surgical operation.</p><p>When you are looking for the best medical sandbags that come with carrying handles, choose one with the following characteristics:</p><p><strong>Should be Infection-resistant material</strong></p><p>When you are selecting the best sandbag for your hospital or clinic, make sure that you choose the one that is made out of fungus and bacteria-resistant materials. It is to ensure that patients who have undergone surgery do not develop any infections.</p><p><strong>Made of Non-magnetic raw materials</strong></p><p>Make sure that the sandbag that you choose is made out of materials that are non-magnetic in nature. The operation theatre or the patient’s recovery room might contain various electronic equipment and influence of magnetic materials might impact the proper functioning. Therefore, non-magnetic sandbags are the best ones to use for this purpose.</p><p><strong>Hypoallergenic</strong></p><p>Since different people are allergic to various elements, it is best to ensure that the <a href='{{< sitepath "product/sandbags/" >}}'><strong>sandbags with handles</strong></a> are made out of materials that have been clinically tested to be hypoallergenic.</p><p><strong>The Option of Different Weights</strong></p><p>Sandbags are available in a range of different weights and dimensions starting from 11 lbs and going up to 20 lbs or more. Make sure to choose the ones according to your requirements.</p><p><strong>Look for Durability</strong></p><p>While buying, make sure that the sandbag that comes with carrying handles are made of reinforced vinyl and contains quality materials. Buying a durable and robust sandbag will make it resilient to easy wear and tear due to regular use.</p><p><strong>Stain-free</strong></p><p>Just like you would always like your hospital premises to remain spick and span and maintain the upholstery to, make sure the sandbags are also clean and tidy. Choose a sandbag that is made out of non-absorbent and stain-resistant fabric, for hygiene purposes.</p><p>So here are some of the essential aspects that you can keep in mind while medical sandbags. Keeping these criteria in focus should help you find the most suitable sandbags for your use. However, care must also be taken to find out that the sandbag that you buy is stitched together properly to avoid leakage of sand. Choose a reliable manufacturer that provides quality sandbags in varying sizes to suit your specific requirements.</p>  <div><div><p>
