<div><p><span>Tempurpedic mattresses are mattresses that are designed to provide relaxation from bedsores and backaches. They are designed in a way that provides proper spinal alignment and back support. While some Tempurpedic mattress is manually controlled, some are electronically powered, and some are a standard version without any control. </span></p>
<p><span>Typically this type of mattress is used in hospitals; however, patients who are bedridden at home also use this mattress. <h2><strong>Why Should You Choose Tempurpedic Mattress?</strong></h2>
<p><span>There are many reasons to invest in Tempurpedic Mattress –</span></p>
<ol>
<li>
<h3><strong>Doesn’t Sink</strong></h3>
</li>
</ol>
<p><span>Over the course of time, standard mattresses tend to sink and create a cavity when you sleep. This is probably one of the worst things to happen that impacts your sleep. However, the Tempurpedic mattress holds its shape and conforms to your body as soon as you lie on it. It is made from TEMPUR-material that provides the right amount of foam and density. Instead of the user adapting to the mattress, the Tempurpedic mattress adapts to users’ weight and shape.</span></p>
<ol>
<li>
<h3><strong>Limited Movements</strong></h3>
<p><span>People need to stay still when you are trying to sleep, but it can be complicated when you are sharing a bed with someone. Tempurpedic mattresses are made of materials that allow you to have a separate sleeping zone where you don’t feel the movement of our sleeping partner. So you can peacefully sleep, irrespective of the constant tossing and turning of your partner.</span></p></li>
</ol>
<ol>
<li>
<h3><strong>The Lack of Springs</strong></h3>
<p><span>Springs in a mattress can be uncomfortable. This is because; springs tend to put a lot of pressure on your back, causing it to ache over the course of time. Tempurpedic mattress doesn’t have springs to push your back. Instead, it provides the necessary support so that it molds to your body perfectly. These mattresses are made from different layers of foam to ensure that it perfectly conforms to the body and distribute body weight equally.</span></p></li>
</ol>
<ol>
<li>
<h3><strong>Comfortable Sleeping Experience</strong></h3>
<p><span>By providing adequate balance and support to the entire body equally, the Tempurpedic mattress facilitates comfortable sleeping experiences. It makes you feel refreshed as you had uninterrupted sleeping time and woke up without backache or body sores.</span></p></li>
</ol>
