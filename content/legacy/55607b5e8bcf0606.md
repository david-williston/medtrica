<figure></figure><p>No matter what you do, you spend a significant 1/3 of your life sleeping. Therefore, having a comfortable bed shouldn’t just be a choice but a must for your overall development. And with the introduction of memory foam mattresses in the consumer market, you can easily make your bed your sleeping sanctuary.</p><p>These gel-infused memory foam mattresses are flexible and conform to the shape of your body to relieve your body parts from excessive pressure. Having shared all the pros of the memory foam mattress, there’s one potential con too of these mattresses – they can be expensive.</p><p><strong>Also Read: <a href='{{< sitepath "how-to-pick-memory-foam-mattress-thickness-the-ultimate-guide/" >}}'>How to Pick Memory Foam Mattress Thickness: The Ultimate Guide</a></strong></p><p>Well, though not every pocket can invest in a <a href='{{< sitepath "product-category/memory-foam-mattress/" >}}'>memory foam mattress</a>, most people can enjoy certain comfort of a memory foam mattress topper.</p><h2><strong><span>Here’s How You can Select the Right Memory Foam Mattress Topper for Your Bed</span></strong></h2><ul>
<li><h3><strong><span>Size Matters for </span></strong><strong><span>Memory Foam Mattress Topper </span></strong></h3>
</li></ul><p>When looking for a memory foam topper for your mattress, you need to check the dimensions of your bed first. Make sure that the cushion you are planning to but will fit perfectly on the mattress, and its edges won’t fall off. The wrong size will not only look clumsy, but it will interrupt in your sleep as well. Plus, they are available in most standard sizes such as Queen, King, California King, Twin, Twin XL, Full, Full XL and the rarest of sizes like the antique ¾ sized mattress out there!</p><ul>
<li><h3><strong><span>Select the Right Density of </span></strong><strong><span>Memory Foam Mattress Topper </span></strong></h3>
</li></ul><p>The density of mattress topper is defined by the weight of the bed per square meter. It declares how firm the mattress is. The better the firmness is, the better is the quality and weight of your mattress topper, and it conforms better to your body shape. Additionally, as the density increases, the advantages of using memory foam such as adjusting to the body heat, durability, and the level of support your body gets also increases.</p><p>High-density mattress toppers weigh 6 pounds per cubic meter and prove excellent for the people who have chronic lower back pain. However, they can be a bit expensive. Moreover, if you are looking for a mattress topper which is softer and lightweight, you can opt for mid-range 3-5 pounds per cubic meter. They are perfect for people planning to add some comfort to their sleep.</p><ul>
<li><h3><strong><span>Choose the Right Thickness of </span></strong><strong><span>Memory Foam Mattress Topper </span></strong></h3>
</li></ul><p>Varying in thickness from 1 to 4 inches, you can select the one according to what will suit your mattress the best. If your bed is already quite comfortable and you need the memory foam mattress topper to add to the life of the mattress, you can opt for a thinner mattress of 2 inches. They are comparatively less expensive and easy to handle. Whereas, if your mattress has become old and you do not want to replace it, a mattress topper of 3 or 4-inch thickness will change the entire feel of your bed. If you are a side sleeper, it will provide support to your body well to save you from soreness in the morning.</p><ul>
<li><h3><strong><span>Hypoallergenic and Waterproof  Toppers are Better than Regular Ones</span></strong></h3>
</li></ul><p>Though <a href='{{< sitepath "product/custom-size-memory-foam-mattress/" >}}'>custom memory foam mattress</a> toppers are supposed to be waterproof and hypoallergenic, you must always confirm before buying that they are. Never invest in a topper which retains dust mites and bacteria which can harm the health of your family.</p><p>Waterproof nature of the mattress toppers protects them from your bodily liquids and spills which deteriorate their durability.</p><ul>
<li><h3><strong><span>You Need to Cover the </span></strong><strong><span>Memory Foam Mattress </span></strong><strong><span>Topper Too</span></strong></h3>
</li></ul><p>Well, while prioritizing the protection and durability of the mattress, we often overlook that the memory foam mattress topper needs some protection too. Though the memory foam mattress toppers are hypoallergenic and waterproof, you need to cover them with cotton covers with a high thread count. This will not only protect your mattress topper and extend its durability, and it will make your bedding look great too.</p><ul>
<li><h3><strong><span>See if the Memory Foam Mattress Topper Can Combat Heat Efficiently</span></strong></h3>
</li></ul><p>Memory foam is one material which has a lot of merits but is often criticized for being a material which can heat up due to the body heat, causing discomfort. Especially in summers, sleeping on memory foam mattress toppers can become uncomfortable. Therefore, with the advancement in technology, there are materials which can be infused into the memory foam to combat overheating. They do this through cells which allow the mattress to breathe. So, make sure that your mattress topper can breathe for better night’s sleep.</p><p><strong>Also Read – <a href='{{< sitepath "enjoy-comfortable-sleep-memory-foam-mattress/" >}}'>Enjoy the Most Comfortable Sleep on Memory Foam Mattress</a></strong></p><p>Memory foam is one material invented and used by NASA for the comfort of the astronauts in the space shuttles. This is one material which can successfully conform to the shape of your body and spring back when not in use, a feature often absent in other mattresses. So, after its success in the 60s and 70s with the space programmes, this was launched for public use. Since then, people are going gaga over this incredible material for a luxurious night’s sleep. Now, with the memory foam mattress toppers, you can enjoy the same experience.</p>  <div><div><p>
